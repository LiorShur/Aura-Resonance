# Privacy — Aura Resonance pilot

Canonical text for the in-app policy (`#privacy`, `src/features/legal/PrivacyScreen.tsx`).
Keep the two in sync. This is a closed research pilot for a small invited group.

**Eligibility.** You must be 16 or older.

**What we collect.**
- Email (sign-in), plus a display name and avatar you choose.
- Age confirmation (that you are 16+) as a yes/no with a date — not your birth date.
- Location **only** when you tap “I’m here” to verify a quest, or when you leave an
  Echo. Never in the background.
- Content you create: quest photos, Echoes, dilemmas and advice, and ratings.
- Basic activity events (app open, quest completion) to measure whether the game works.

**What we do not collect.** No background/continuous location. No health, heart-rate,
or fitness data. No contacts, ad identifiers, or third-party trackers.

**Photos.** Screened, and any faces are automatically blurred before storage. The
original unblurred photo is never kept.

**Content handling.** Dilemmas and advice are shown to a few other players, anonymous
between author and adviser. Echoes are visible near where they were left. Everything
is checked by an automated safety filter before it’s visible; content suggesting a
crisis is routed to support resources instead of being shared.

**Retention.**
- Precise check-in coordinates: 30 days, then blurred to ~1km for aggregate stats.
- Dilemmas and advice: deleted 90 days after they close.
- Echoes: expire after 30 days.

**Deletion.** Self-service at any time (Profile → Danger zone): removes your account,
content, and location records immediately. No email request needed.

**Contact.** The pilot organiser, at the address you were invited from.
